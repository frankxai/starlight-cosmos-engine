# Ontology and provenance boundary

The Cosmos Engine produces factual, interpretive, and mythic content. Those modes may coexist in one artifact only when every consequential claim remains classifiable.

## Canon dependency

The authoritative worldview target is `frankxai/starlight-canon`. Until that repository exists and a version is pinned, the engine uses this bootstrap invariant:

- Starlight is stellar light and a metaphor/principle of valuable transmission.
- It is not a deity, supernatural species, omniscient AI, revelation, Source, or Arcanean entity.
- Real religious concepts remain inside their traditions; comparison preserves non-equivalence.
- Arcanea is fiction. Its Source, Lumina, Nero, Field, Guardians, Godbeasts, Shinkami, Luminor, and frequencies are not established physics or historical religion.

## Publish gate

Every publishable artifact declares exactly one primary provenance class:

- historical source claim
- scholarly interpretation
- Starlight interpretation
- original Starlight philosophy
- original literary/mythic material
- Arcanea fiction
- mixed, with claim-level boundaries

Historical and scholarly claims require source IDs. Original Starlight interpretations require claim IDs. Fiction requires a visible fiction boundary. Human approval is mandatory before publish.

The `ContentProvenance` contract and `isPublishableProvenance` validator encode this minimum. They do not replace editorial review.
