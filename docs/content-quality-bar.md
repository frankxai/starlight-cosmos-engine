# Content Quality Bar

Content ships only when all quality dimensions pass defined thresholds:

- Factual accuracy and claim-to-source traceability
- Ontology integrity against `docs/ontology-and-provenance-boundary.md`
- Visible separation of history, scholarship, interpretation, philosophy, literature, and Arcanea fiction
- Narrative clarity without tradition-flattening or scientific overclaim
- Visual coherence
- Platform-fit readiness
- Rights completeness
- Human approval for publishable provenance

A compelling story does not compensate for an invented historical claim. A source does not license a stronger conclusion than it supports.
